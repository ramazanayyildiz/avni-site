Stays YouTube Music

  • Sign-in & accountYou sign in to YouTube Music in its own window. Avni never sees your password.
  • Library & playlistsYour playlists stream in from your library, and long ones keep loading as you scroll.
  • AudioPlayback runs in YouTube Music. The Classic player mirrors its state.

Becomes yours

  • Classic or StandardPixel skin or a clean modern layout. Switch at any time.
  • SkinsThree original skins, plus local .wsz import. 1× or 2× size.
  • PanelsLibrary, Playlist, Now Playing, Appearance and Account panels close and reopen, and the layout survives a restart.
  • Jump to FileType a few letters to jump to any track in a loaded playlist.

Skin kit

Five colours make a skin.

Avni's original skins use a five-colour palette and classic .wsz format. The app accepts compatible .wsz skins; unsupported formats and skin features may not work.

The app includes three original skins and supports local import. It does not include third-party historical skin packs.

Known limitations
  • Only compatible classic .wsz skins are supported; .wal skins are not.
  • YouTube Music owns audio playback. Audio equalization is not supported; Avni does not expose an equalizer.
  • Avni has no cloud library or sync. Appearance preferences and imported skins are stored locally; recent searches and playback history are not persisted by the current app.
  • Google and YouTube receive requests needed for sign-in, library, playback and artwork under their own policies.
Build from source

The application source is not published yet, so visitors cannot currently build Avni from source. The project has not selected a source license. Internal development docs describe a local macOS build using Node.js/npm, Rust, Xcode Command Line Tools and the Tauri prerequisites; that build is an unsigned debug app, not a release installer. A browser preview does not provide native playback or skin-import behavior.

FAQ

What the app does today.

Where do sign-in and audio happen?

You sign in to YouTube Music in its own app web view. YouTube Music handles the account and audio; Avni mirrors playback state and presents library controls.

Does Avni support audio equalization?

No. YouTube Music owns audio playback and Avni does not modify its audio or expose an equalizer.

Can I use old Winamp skins?

You can import a compatible classic .wsz skin from your device. Avni includes three original skins and does not bundle historical third-party skin collections.

Does Avni store my library in the cloud?

No Avni account, cloud library or sync service is provided. Appearance settings, panel visibility and imported skin files are stored locally. The current app does not persist recent searches or playback history. Google and YouTube still receive requests for sign-in, library, playback and artwork.

Privacy

Your provider session stays with YouTube Music.

Avni does not ask for your Google password or send it to an Avni server. WebKit manages the YouTube Music session on your device. Google and YouTube receive requests needed for sign-in, library, playback and artwork under their own policies.

Avni has no account, cloud sync or external analytics endpoint. Startup timings are measured locally and may appear in the app process log. Remove imported skins in Appearance; clearing Avni's app-local data removes appearance settings and skin files. Classic panel visibility is stored in the app UI's local web storage. Sign out in YouTube Music to clear its separate WebKit session. Concept site repository and project status.
