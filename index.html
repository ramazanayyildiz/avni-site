How it fits together

The music stays where it is.
Only the look changes.

Stays YouTube Music

  • Sign-in & accountYou sign in to YouTube Music in its own window. Avni never sees your password.
  • Library & playlistsYour playlists stream in from your library, and long ones keep loading as you scroll.
  • AudioPlayback runs in YouTube Music. The Classic player mirrors its state.

Becomes yours

  • Classic or StandardPixel skin or a clean modern layout. Switch at any time.
  • SkinsThree original skins, plus local .wsz import. 1× or 2× size.
  • PanelsLibrary, Playlist, Now Playing, Appearance and Account panels close and reopen, and the layout survives a restart.
  • Jump to FileType a few letters to jump to any track in a loaded playlist.

Skin kit

Five colours make a skin.

Each skin in Avni is drawn from a five-colour palette by a small Python script with no dependencies. Change the colours, run the script, and import the .wsz it builds.

$ python3 tools/skin-kit/build.py
# builds graphite.wsz, silver.wsz and amber.wsz
# Appearance → Import .wsz skin → choose your file

Try it

In active development.

This page previews Avni's design. The macOS app is still being tested and is not available as a public download yet.

The app will let you sign in through YouTube Music and browse your library in a classic, skinnable interface.
